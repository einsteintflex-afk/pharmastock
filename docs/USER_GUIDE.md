# User Guide

Open PharmaStock in a browser (`https://your-server/app/`). On a phone or tablet use the
☰ button for the menu; the app can be **installed** from the browser menu ("Add to home
screen"). What you see depends on your role.

## Signing in

Enter your username and password. First time: choose a new password (10+ characters,
letters and numbers). With **two-step verification** on, enter the 6-digit code from your
authenticator app (or one of your recovery codes). Forgot your password? **Forgot your
password?** sends a reset link if your account has an e-mail address; otherwise ask an
administrator.

Turn on two-step verification under **My account → Two-step verification**: scan the QR
code with Google Authenticator / Microsoft Authenticator / Authy, enter the code, and keep
the 10 recovery codes somewhere safe.

## Command Center

Quick actions (new sale, scan, receive, count, adjust, reorder…), **What needs
attention** (most urgent first, each with a link to fix it) and the **Daily brief**.
Press **/** anywhere to search medicines, batches, orders, sales and more.

## Scan Center

Choose a mode, then scan with the phone camera (Chrome / Edge on Android) or a scanner:
**Look up**, **Receive** (against an open order, or as opening stock), **Count** (continue
an open stock count), **Sell**, **Adjust**, **Transfer**. An unknown barcode shows
**PRODUCT NOT FOUND → Create new medicine** with the barcode filled in; you check every
detail before saving.

## Dispensing counter

1. Type a medicine name — or **scan the pack barcode** — and press Enter.
2. Set quantity, price and directions. Batches are chosen automatically, earliest expiry
   first (FEFO); the batches used are shown under each line. Expired, quarantined and
   recalled stock is never dispensed.
3. Choose OTC or prescription (prescriber and prescription number), payment method, an
   optional **discount** (amount or %), and — only if the customer agrees — tick
   WhatsApp / SMS / e-mail to send them the receipt. **Complete dispensing**.
4. Print the receipt, download it as **PDF** or **Thermal (80 mm)**, or copy the
   **digital receipt link**. A wrong sale can be **voided** from Sales History
   (pharmacists): the stock returns to the same batches.

## Receiving stock

- **Purchasing**: create an order for a supplier, add lines, then **Receive** as deliveries
  arrive (scan the pack's DataMatrix code to fill in batch and expiry). Partial deliveries
  are fine.
- **Register batch** (Inventory): stock that did not come through an order (opening stock,
  donations).

## Expiry and stock

- **Expiry Alerts**: expired, critical, urgent and approaching batches. Write off expired
  stock when it is disposed of.
- **Inventory**: every batch with status, hold status and value. On a batch: history with
  running balance, **Quarantine** / **Recall** (removes it from dispensing) and **Release**.
- **Stock Movements**: every change, filterable, exportable.
- **Reconciliation**: shows whether every batch matches its movement history.
- **Barcode Scan**: scan any pack to see the medicine, its batches, the FEFO batch, open
  orders and warnings (expired pack, expiry mismatch, batch on hold).

## Stock counts and adjustments

- **Stock Counts → New count**, choose the location, then scan each pack ("add to count")
  or type the total on the shelf ("set count to"). Differences show in units and value.
  **Submit for review**; a manager posts the count and the stock is corrected.
- **Adjustments**: breakage, damage, theft / loss, corrections… with a reason code; large
  adjustments wait for a manager's approval.

## Reorder

**Reorder** lists what to buy, with safety stock, reorder point and suggested quantity;
tick the lines and **Create purchase order** (last price paid is used).

## Transfers and requisitions

**Transfers** (hospitals: **Requisitions**): request stock from another location, see
approval, dispatch and receipt. Approvers approve or reject; the store dispatches; the
receiving location confirms receipt.

## Analytics, reports and assistant

- **Command Center**: attention list, daily brief, key figures, alerts, purchases.
- **Analytics**: reorder recommendations (with the calculation), expiry risk, consumption,
  trends, stock by location, stock-outs, forecast (with data sufficiency and confidence),
  turnover, valuation, supplier performance.
- **Reports**: 26 reports (incl. out of stock, fast-moving, stock counts, adjustments), on
  screen or as CSV, Excel or PDF with your company logo.
- **AI Assistant**: ask in plain language ("What needs reordering?", "What will run out
  this week?", "Today's priorities", "Which supplier orders are outstanding?", "Show the
  history of Amoxicillin"). Answers come only from
  your data; the assistant gives inventory decision support, never clinical advice.

## Notifications and your account

The bell shows unread notifications (expiry, low stock, deliveries, transfers). Under
**My Account** change your password, choose e-mail / SMS notifications, and see or sign
out your devices.
